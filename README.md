# FILTEROUT-Review-Decision-Support
A consumer-focused prototype exploring how online shoppers can interpret product review signals to make more informed purchasing decisions.
## Problem

Online shoppers often rely on product reviews when making purchasing decisions, but determining which reviews to trust can be difficult.

## Product Concept

FILTEROUT explores a review decision-support experience that presents review-related signals in a simple, visual format.

The goal is to help shoppers understand relevant information without having to manually interpret large amounts of review content.

## Key Features

- **Confidence Indicator:** Presents a visual summary of review confidence.
- **Positive Signals:** Highlights indicators that may support confidence in reviews.
- **Warning Indicators:** Draws attention to signals that may require further consideration.
- **Simplified Information:** Focuses on information relevant to a purchasing decision.

## My Approach

The idea came from exploring consumer concerns about online review trustworthiness and seeing a discussion about the challenges shoppers face.

I explored how review-related information could be organized into a more understandable decision-support interface.


## Project Status

**Independent concept prototype.**

The prototype explores how review signals could be presented to shoppers. It has not been validated through structured user testing, and the confidence indicators should not be interpreted as proof that a review is genuine or fake.

## Future Improvements

- Test whether shoppers understand the indicators.
- Identify which review signals users find most useful.
- Gather feedback on the clarity of the interface.
- Explore how review analysis could be implemented and evaluated reliably.
